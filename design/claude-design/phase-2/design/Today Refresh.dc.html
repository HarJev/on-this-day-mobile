4Component changes
Masthead
56 tall, 3-column grid (72 · 1fr · 72). Serif 22/600 title centred, short date right (14/600). Stone hairline below. There are no icons in it.
Featured card
Eyebrow row: "Featured" cobalt, full weekday date muted. Ivory card, radius 16. The image is full-bleed at 16:9.5, or matted (tweak). The year is serif 40 cobalt, with the place in copper-dark 13/600. A copper hairline at 55%, then a serif 28/1.15 title, a 16/1.5 summary and "Read the full story →". With no image, the year grows to 56 and the title to 30.
Event row
Grid 52 · 1fr · 22, gap 12, min 60, stone divider. Year is serif 17/600 cobalt, so years align down the page. The title is 16/1.4 and wraps fully. Muted chevron.
Recent day row
Same grid with a 64 date column: "Aug 21" 14/600 cobalt over a 12 weekday ("Today" for the current day, in ink). The title is prefixed by its year in muted text.
Section header
Serif 22/600 with a 32×2 copper bar underneath. This replaces the full-width copper underline.
Image + credit
Detail image at natural aspect, radius 8, 1px stone border (or matted). The credit sits directly below as a 12/1.4 muted caption, with no disclosure.
Sources disclosure
Same component as the quiz: a single 52px "Sources (n)" row between stone hairlines. It expands in place; each link row is min 52 with the publisher (15/600), the title (13 muted) and a cobalt open_in_new icon.
Quiz link
Optional. A 64px ivory row, radius 14, with a 40px cobalt-tint circle quiz icon, "Test what you learned" and a chevron. It sits after sources.
State block
Ivory card, radius 16: a 44px icon circle (fill for empty, copper tint for error), a serif 24 title, a 15 body and at most one primary action (plus Back on detail). Empty uses a dashed border.
Skeleton
Fill-colour (#EBE3D6) blocks, radius 6, matching the real layout. They pulse in opacity 1 → 0.6 over 1.2 s and stay static under reduced motion. Content fades in over 150 ms.
Notification explainer
A bottom sheet shown before the OS prompt: a notifications icon in a cobalt-tint circle, a serif 26 title, one sentence, a preview of the notification, then "Enable daily history reminder" (primary) and "Not now" (text). "Not now" doesn't trigger the OS prompt.
Tab bar
Unchanged from Phase 1: the active tab has a 56×28 cobalt-tint pill, a filled icon and a 600 label.
